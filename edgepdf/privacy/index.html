01. Introduction & Commitment

This Privacy Policy explains how EdgePDF SmartTools - 1-Click Table to Excel & OCR ("we", "our", or "the extension") protects user privacy across Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, and compatible Chromium browsers.

We believe your sensitive business documents, research papers, financial invoices, and medical records belong solely to you. EdgePDF SmartTools is architected from the ground up as a client-side document utility: all table extraction algorithms, optical character recognition (OCR) routines, and spreadsheet generation engines run locally within your browser sandbox.

02. Data We Never Collect

We do not collect, intercept, record, sell, or disclose your personal information. Specifically, EdgePDF SmartTools does NOT collect:

  • Document Files & PDF Content: PDFs opened in your browser or dropped into EdgePDF Studio are never uploaded to any remote server operated by us.
  • Extracted Table Data: Tables converted into Microsoft Excel (.xlsx) or CSV are generated in local computer memory.
  • Browsing Activity & URLs: We do not monitor your web browsing history, bookmarks, or web activity.
  • Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • Telemetry & Usage Trackers: We do not run Google Analytics, Sentry, Mixpanel, or background telemetry pings.

03. Data Stored On Your Local Device

EdgePDF SmartTools utilizes your browser's private local storage (chrome.storage.local) to persist settings locally on your machine:

  • Toolbar & UI Preferences: Position and visibility toggles for the floating PDF toolbar and high-contrast dark reader mode.
  • Daily Free Export Counter: A timestamp and integer counting free table exports per 24-hour cycle.
  • Local Export History: Recent conversion metadata (file name, table row count, export timestamp) saved locally so you can easily review your recent work.
  • Document Notes: User annotations and notes created in the side-by-side reader, stored locally per document URL or file hash.
  • Optional BYOK AI Keys: If you choose to configure cloud AI features with your own personal OpenAI or Google Gemini API key, that key is stored strictly on your local machine and is never transmitted to us.
ðŸ’¡ Data Reset: You can purge all cached notes, preferences, and keys at any time via the EdgePDF Settings drawer or by uninstalling the extension.

04. Browser Permissions Justification

In adherence to the Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Chrome Web Store Least Privilege Principle, EdgePDF SmartTools requests only the minimum permissions essential for document processing:

Permission Scope Why It Is Strictly Required
storage Local Sandbox Storage Required to persist UI preferences, local export history, notes, and optional personal AI API keys locally on your device.
activeTab Active Tab Inspection Required to access and extract tables or OCR regions from the currently open PDF or web page only when explicitly triggered by you (via button, hotkey, or context menu).
scripting Content Script Injection Required to inject the interactive area snip crop box and floating PDF action toolbar into the active tab.
contextMenus Right-Click Shortcuts Enables quick context-menu shortcuts (e.g. "Export Table to Excel", "Area Snip OCR") directly when right-clicking on pages.
api.gumroad.com Pro License Verification Used strictly to verify license keys against Gumroad's secure licensing API if you activate an EdgePDF Pro license.
api.openai.com
generativelanguage.googleapis.com		 Optional BYOK AI Endpoints Enables direct communication between your browser and OpenAI or Google Gemini only if you provide your own personal API key for AI document summarization.

05. Third-Party Services & APIs

EdgePDF SmartTools connects only to external third-party services in the following specific, user-initiated circumstances:

  • Gumroad Licensing (api.gumroad.com): When you purchase EdgePDF Pro and input your license key, a secure HTTPS call verifies your key with Gumroad. No document data or browsing history is included.
  • OpenAI / Google Gemini (Optional BYOK): If you choose to enable cloud AI summarization using your own personal API key, text snippets you request to summarize are transmitted directly from your browser to OpenAI or Google's endpoints over encrypted TLS. Please review OpenAI Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

06. Remote Code Compliance

EdgePDF SmartTools complies fully with Manifest V3 security requirements:

  • All libraries, including PDF.js, SheetJS (xlsx), and OCR canvas processors, are packaged locally inside the extension.
  • No external scripts, remote code, or CDN-hosted dependencies are fetched or executed at runtime.

07. Data Retention & Erasure

We maintain no remote user databases or account servers. All data remains exclusively on your personal device. Uninstalling the extension immediately wipes all associated local storage and preferences.

08. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under GDPR and CCPA, you have complete sovereignty over your data. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your information. You can inspect or delete all local data at any time.

09. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

EdgePDF SmartTools is a general audience document productivity utility. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
