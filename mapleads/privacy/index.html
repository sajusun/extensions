01. Introduction & Commitment

This Privacy Policy explains how MapLeads Pro - Google Maps Lead Scraper & B2B Exporter ("we", "our", or "the extension") handles data across Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, and compatible Chromium browsers.

MapLeads Pro is built for sales professionals, marketing agencies, and freelance developers looking to build local business outreach lists. Unlike third-party cloud scrapers that siphon your leads and search keywords into their proprietary databases, MapLeads Pro runs completely client-side. The extension parses Google Maps search results in your active browser session and converts them into structured spreadsheets without server intermediaries.

02. Data We Never Collect

We believe your lead generation strategy and prospect lists are your proprietary business assets. MapLeads Pro does NOT collect, record, transmit, sell, or disclose:

  • Scraped Business Leads: Names, phone numbers, addresses, emails, and ratings extracted from Google Maps are kept in your browser and saved directly to your computer.
  • Search Keywords & Locations: We do not monitor or log what business categories, cities, or postal codes you search.
  • Browsing Activity & URLs: We do not track websites you visit outside the active Google Maps scraping workflow.
  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect your name, email, payment card, or phone number.
  • Telemetry & Usage Analytics: We do not integrate Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or telemetry tracking pixels.

03. Data Stored On Your Local Device

MapLeads Pro uses chrome.storage.local exclusively on your machine to support seamless workflows across browser sessions:

  • Current Scrape Queue: Extracted leads currently pending export in the UI table preview.
  • Scraping Preferences: Scroll delay configuration, deduplication toggles, and "No Website" filter settings.
  • Daily Free Quota Counter: A local integer and timestamp tracking daily free usage (50 leads per 24 hours).
  • Export History: Timestamps and filenames of recent exports so you can review your session metrics locally.
ðŸ’¡ Full User Control: You can wipe all stored leads and reset settings at any moment by clicking "Clear Queue" in the extension popup or by uninstalling the extension.

04. Browser Permissions Justification

In accordance with Chrome Web Store and Microsoft Edge Add-ons Minimum Privileges Principle, MapLeads Pro requests only the permissions necessary to scrape and export lead lists:

Permission Scope Why It Is Strictly Required
storage Local Sandbox Storage Required to store extracted lead records, user scraping configurations, and daily quota counts locally on your machine.
activeTab Active Tab Interaction Required to interact with the currently open Google Maps search results tab when you click Start Scraping.
scripting Content Script Injection Required to execute the humanized auto-scrolling routine and DOM element extractor on the active Google Maps page.
clipboardWrite TSV Clipboard Copy Enables the 1-click "Copy TSV" button so you can instantly paste extracted leads into Google Sheets, Excel, or Airtable.
downloads Direct File Export Required to trigger the direct browser download of generated Microsoft Excel (.xlsx) and CSV files onto your device.

05. Public Data & Web Scraping Standards

MapLeads Pro only extracts information that is made publicly accessible by businesses on Google Maps or on their public websites. The extension respects rate limits through randomized human-like scrolling pauses (Gaussian physics) to prevent server strain.

06. Remote Code Compliance

MapLeads Pro complies fully with Manifest V3 security mandates:

  • Zero remote code. All libraries, including SheetJS (xlsx), are bundled locally in the extension package.
  • No scripts or styles are loaded dynamically from external third-party servers.

07. Data Retention & Erasure

We do not store your data on external servers. Uninstalling the extension from your browser (chrome://extensions) permanently erases all locally saved leads and configurations.

08. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under GDPR and CCPA, you retain complete data sovereignty. We collect no personal information from our users and do not sell or monetize personal data.

09. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

MapLeads Pro is a commercial B2B sales productivity tool and does not collect information from anyone under the age of 13.
