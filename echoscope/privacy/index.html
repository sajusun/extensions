01. Introduction & Purpose

This Privacy Policy explains the data privacy practices of EchoScope - Laravel Reverb & WebSocket Inspector ("we", "our", or "the extension") across all supported Chromium browsers.

EchoScope is built for backend and full-stack software engineers debugging real-time broadcast events, Laravel Reverb, Pusher-compatible protocols, and WebSocket servers. The tool functions 100% client-side: all network handshakes, event streaming logs, and channel authentication requests occur solely between your browser and the specific endpoints you configure.

02. Data We Never Collect

We do not collect, monitor, store, or sell any user information. Specifically, EchoScope does NOT collect:

  • Broadcast Event Payloads: Incoming and outgoing WebSocket messages, event names, and JSON payloads remain strictly in your browser session memory and are never uploaded to any cloud server.
  • Browsing Activity & History: We do not log which websites you visit or intercept general web traffic.
  • Credentials & Secrets: Your application keys, secrets, and auth bearer tokens are kept strictly in your local device sandbox.
  • Analytics & Usage Telemetry: We do not embed Google Analytics, PostHog, Mixpanel, or crash-reporting telemetry beacons.

03. Data Stored On Your Local Device

EchoScope utilizes chrome.storage.local exclusively to persist your developer connection profiles so you do not have to retype them upon closing and reopening the extension.

Locally stored items include:

  • Connection Profiles: Host addresses (e.g. 127.0.0.1, reverb.localhost), port numbers, app keys, and scheme selection (ws / wss).
  • Channel Subscriptions: List of public, private, or presence channels you have chosen to subscribe to (e.g., orders.123).
  • Auth Endpoints: URL of your Laravel broadcasting auth route (e.g. /broadcasting/auth) and optional custom headers.
  • Local Event Log Buffer: Recent message history stored temporarily in local memory to allow filtering and payload inspection.
💡 Resetting Data: You can wipe all stored connection profiles and message logs with one click inside EchoScope settings or by clearing your browser's extension data.

04. Browser Permissions Justification

EchoScope requests only the absolute minimum permissions needed to inspect WebSocket events and authenticate channels:

Permission Scope Why It Is Strictly Required
storage Local Sandbox Storage Required to save connection configurations (host, port, app key, custom headers, subscribed channels) on your machine.
activeTab Tab Management Required when you click the popout inspector button to open the full-screen EchoScope console in a dedicated browser tab.
host_permissions (<all_urls>) WebSocket & Auth Network Access Required to establish WebSocket connections (ws://*, wss://*) and send HTTP POST authorization requests (http://*, https://*) to user-specified local developer servers (e.g., ws://127.0.0.1:8080, http://localhost:8000/broadcasting/auth) across any domain you test.

05. Remote Code Compliance

In accordance with Chrome Web Store and Microsoft Edge Manifest V3 security requirements:

  • EchoScope contains zero remote scripts. No JavaScript, WebAssembly, or third-party stylesheets are fetched from external servers at runtime.
  • All protocol logic, WebSocket reconnect handlers, and JSON syntax highlighters are bundled and packaged locally inside the extension.

06. Third-Party Disclosure

We do not transfer, sell, or disclose user data to third parties, advertisers, or analytics brokers. Network calls occur solely between your client and your designated WebSocket and authorization servers.

07. Data Retention & Erasure

We host no external backend servers or user accounts. When you uninstall EchoScope from your browser (chrome://extensions), your browser immediately and permanently erases all locally saved connection profiles and event caches.

08. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

In compliance with GDPR and CCPA regulations, you have absolute control over your data. All configurations are stored locally on your device, and you may inspect, export, or delete them at any time.

09. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

EchoScope is a specialized developer tool and does not collect personal information from individuals of any age, including children under 13.
