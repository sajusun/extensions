How Zero-Server Sync Works

Most developer tools require you to create an account, store your snippets on their company servers, and pay monthly subscriptions. SnapSnippet Pro takes a completely different path.

Using Microsoft Edge and Google Chrome's native chrome.storage.sync infrastructure, your snippets roam across your signed-in browser profiles automatically. We run zero servers and store zero copies of your data.

1
Add Snippet on Work PC: Saved into your browser's encrypted roaming partition.
2
Browser Profile Sync: Edge/Chrome securely mirrors data to your signed-in profile.
3
Instant Availability on Laptop: Open SnapSnippet on your laptop — snippets are already there.
4
Zero External Servers: No third-party databases, no monthly recurring fees.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which browsers are supported?

SnapSnippet runs on Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi, Arc, and any other Chromium-based web browser.

How do I activate my Pro license key?

After purchasing on Gumroad, you receive a license key (e.g., SNIP-PRO-XXXXX). Open SnapSnippet, click the Pro & Cloud Sync icon, paste your key, and click Activate. Activation verifies once via Gumroad API and enables Pro permanently.

Is the $4.99 fee a subscription?

No! SnapSnippet Pro is a one-time lifetime payment of $4.99 USD. You will never be billed again, and all future updates are included.

Can I customize the global shortcut key?

Yes. By default it is Ctrl + Shift + K (or Cmd + Shift + K on macOS). You can customize it at any time by visiting edge://extensions/shortcuts or chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
