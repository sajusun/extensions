🚀 Quick 3-Step Setup Guide

1

Download & Extract the Package

Download snapdisplay-second-monitor.zip and extract the folder anywhere on your computer (e.g. Downloads or Desktop).

2

Run SnapDisplay-Companion.exe

Double-click SnapDisplay-Companion.exe (or right-click → Run as Administrator). Click "⚡ Activate Virtual Display". Windows will stage the driver and instantly initialize Screen 2.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need this companion if I already have a physical second monitor connected to my PC?

No! If Windows already detects 2 physical displays, you do not need this companion app at all. SnapDisplay's smart detection will automatically detect your displays and let you stream Screen 2 immediately.

How do I turn off the virtual display when I'm done?

Simply open SnapDisplay-Companion.exe and click "❌ Turn Off Virtual Display". Windows will return to your single primary screen instantly.

Why did you choose IddCx instead of mirroring?

Screen mirroring only duplicates your main screen. IddCx creates a real separate workspace so you can watch a video or monitor chat on your phone while working full-screen on your PC monitor.
